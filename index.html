THE DETAILS

A little about geotagging

01

What is photo geotagging?

Geotagging adds geographic coordinates to a photo's metadata. It can help you remember where a moment happened and organize images by place.

02

How does GPS metadata work?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP photos can store latitude and longitude in EXIF GPS tags. Coordinates are encoded as degrees, minutes, and seconds with a direction reference.

03

How to geotag a photo

Choose one or more photos, select a map location or enter coordinates, then write and verify the GPS tags before downloading individually or together in a ZIP.

04

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Image data stays in your browser. Only a place name or coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's geocoding service when you search or request a location label.
